PASS 0 FIND YOUR WAY The store has offset departments, side routes and concealed shelves. Search behind displays and inside stock cabinets.
PASS 0 NEARBY MAP The map follows you and shows your room and nearby departments. It follows the selected player while spectating.
PASS 0 PLAYER COLOURS Each arrow and through-wall nickname share a unique colour, kept across rounds and reconnects. Carts: yellow squares. READY: green R.
PASS 0 LIVE PIG & ITEMS The side pig mirrors your appearance and outfit. Aim at items for an E prompt; crouch to inspect low shelves.

PASS 2 THREE SHARED CARTS A cart has one driver and one passenger. Wall collisions stop it; a launched cart can knock down other pigs.
PASS 2 EIGHT STORAGE SLOTS Cargo carries spare garments, including several pieces for the same clothing slot. Transfer items while the cart is stopped or moving slowly.
PASS 2 ONE MONKEY PER PLAYER Toys spawn at random reachable spots. One successful hit consumes a toy, even against an ordinary mannequin; a miss keeps it.
PASS 2 THIRTY-SECOND STUN A toy hit knocks an aggressive mannequin down for 30 seconds. You need free hands and cannot carry a toy while using a cart.

PASS 3 PROXIMITY VOICE Full volume nearby, fading to silence at 25 metres. Your microphone is active only while B is held; allow microphone access when prompted.
PASS 3 WATCH THE MANNEQUINS Aggressive mannequins freeze while a living player sees them. Coordinate who keeps watch while friends search.

PASS 4 ONE CAMERA PER PLAYER Cameras spawn at random spots in the store. Pick one up with E, then use K to equip or stow it. Stowing frees your hands for toys and carts.
PASS 4 CHEAP CAMERA PHOTOS Pictures have chunky pixels, grain and compression artefacts. The viewfinder marks the photo area; the shutter needs 1 second.
PASS 4 ROUND PHOTO ALBUM LMB takes a photo; P opens your latest 12 pictures. Photos exclude the HUD and stay in your local album until the round ends, even after death.
PASS 4 FLASH & RANDOM SPAWNS The flash is cosmetic; it never stuns mannequins. Everyone sees the same random clothing, mannequins, carts, toys and cameras. The entrance stays clear.
